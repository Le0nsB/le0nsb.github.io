SEC. 01 — PROFILE

I'm a programming student from Latvia who likes making things that are actually useful to me. I usually start with some random idea that I think would be interesting and then eventually turn it into an actual project.

I like experimenting with different things, whether it's programming, hardware, or just figuring out how something works. I don't always know exactly what I'm doing when I start, but that's kind of the point I learn as I go and keep messing with it until it works.

I'm especially interested in projects where I can combine programming with something that's personal to me and reflects who I am.
